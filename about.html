About Me

Hello! Assalamualaikum, I am Nursyafiqah Binti Abu Hassan.

I am a final-year student in the Informatics Media program at Universiti Sultan Zainal Abidin (UniSZA).

The Informatics Media field focuses on the integration of technology, creativity and digital media development to create interactive and user-friendly applications and systems.

Throughout my studies, I have developed various skills including web development, user interface (UI) design, digital media management, application development, 3D modelling and software development. I have gained experience in creating interactive digital content, designing user-friendly interfaces and developing creative solutions through different academic projects and practical activities. Through these learning experiences, I continuously strive to enhance my knowledge and technical skills in order to produce innovative and high-quality digital products.
